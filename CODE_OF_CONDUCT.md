# Community code of conduct

Guided CMS should be a welcoming place for users with different backgrounds and levels of technical experience.

## Expected behavior

- be respectful, patient and constructive
- describe problems without attacking people
- welcome questions asked in good faith
- respect privacy, licences and intellectual property
- share only material you are allowed to publish
- keep support conversations relevant and actionable

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, spam, credential sharing, publishing private information, or redistributing commercial or private files is not accepted.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to **palareas@gmail.com**. Do not use a public Issue when a report contains personal or sensitive information.
